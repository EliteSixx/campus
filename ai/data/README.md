# Synthetic placement data

These CSVs are generated by `python data/generate_data.py` from the `ai/` directory. The generator uses Python's `random.Random(42)` and writes deterministic records with `is_synthetic=true` throughout. Do not mix this training data with real student records.

## Files and assumptions

- `students.csv`: 1,500 students over CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, ME, and CE. Branch shares are weighted 26%, 19%, 17%, 12%, 14%, and 12%. CGPA is sampled from a normal distribution with mean 7.2 and standard deviation 0.9, then clipped to 5–10 and rounded to two decimals. Backlogs use probabilities 72%, 18%, 7.5%, and 2.5% for 0–3. Skills are sampled from branch-related and general skills; projects use student skills. Aptitude and communication use CGPA-related means with independent noise. Mock interview scores are null for 32% of students.
- `jobs.csv`: 60 jobs with branch eligibility, 3–6 required skills, three preferred skills, CGPA minimums from 6.0–8.0, backlog limits, company, and CTC.
- `applications.csv`: students apply to 2–8 jobs from their branch for which they meet the stated CGPA/backlog criteria. Students with no eligible jobs have no applications. Shortlisting depends moderately on required-skill overlap and CGPA with substantial random noise; 17% of shortlist outcomes are flipped to create contradictory/noisy cases.
- `interviews.csv`: shortlisted applicants attend with probability related to skill overlap. Attended interview scores combine communication, mock score (55 when missing), and noise.
- `offers.csv`: attended interviews receive offers according to skill overlap, CGPA, mock score, and substantial noise. 17% of offer outcomes are flipped. Offer acceptance is a separate 90% random decision, allowing outcomes to vary beyond profile features. This creates unplaced cases even among some strong candidates and placed cases among some weaker candidates.

List-valued CSV cells (`skills`, `projects`, and eligibility/skill fields) use JSON encoding. Missing mock scores and interview scores are empty CSV cells. Application and interview/offer IDs are deterministic sequence IDs. The target used by the risk model is 1 when the student has no accepted offer.

## Generated placement funnel

With seed 42, 73.33% of students remain unplaced (no accepted offer). The following rates show the share of all 1,500 students who reached each stage at least once: applied 82.33%, shortlisted 57.87%, attended an interview 50.07%, received an offer 28.93%, and accepted an offer 26.67%. These are cohort reach rates, so a student can contribute once to each stage even when applying to multiple drives.

## Model evaluation
Selected **gradient_boosting** by ROC-AUC. Stratified test metrics: accuracy 0.7733, precision 0.8193, recall 0.8864, F1 0.8515, ROC-AUC 0.8568. Confusion matrix (actual rows [placed, unplaced], predicted columns [placed, unplaced]): `[[37, 43], [25, 195]]`.
