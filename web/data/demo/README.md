# CampusLink demo seed

The records in this folder are isolated demo seeds. `ST001`, `ST104`, and `JOB001` match the published contract examples; custom drive, application, and offer records use local TypeScript types because the AI contract does not define those business entities.

## Demo story

1. **Profile:** Open the ten student profiles, starting with strong backend candidate ST001 and at-risk candidate ST104.
2. **Readiness:** Compare ST001's backend skills and project evidence with ST104's lower CGPA, active backlogs, and assessment scores.
3. **JD parse:** Parse the Acme Tech Backend Developer role and inspect its required Python, SQL, REST APIs, and Docker skills.
4. **Ranking:** Rank the varied student profiles against JOB001 to show why ST001 rises to the top and which skills remain preferred.
5. **Conflict:** Schedule the two drives on October 22, 2026, from 10:00 to 12:00. They deliberately share ST001 and ST008, Seminar Hall 1, and Panel A, so student, venue, and panel conflicts are visible. The slot falls within the requested October 19–30 free window, before the supplied November 2–9 Class Test II and November 20–December 19 exam periods.
6. **Offer and at-risk follow-up:** Show ST001's Acme Tech offer at 8 LPA, July 1, 2027 joining date, and document checklist; then review ST104's activity of six applications, zero shortlists, and six rejections.

The official [BPUT academic calendar page](https://www.bput.ac.in/academic-calendar.html) lists the Odd Semester 2026–27 calendar. The exact assessment and examination dates above are the dates supplied for this demo's scheduling window.
